Just yesterday, someone asked me why Clojure. This article is exactly the answer I wish I'd had. But more importantly, I think the first paragraph summarises my view on working with large language models. The complexity of a project still has to be managed by the engineer, since LLMs struggle to reason consistently about entire systems. The most important skill in software engineering is no longer coding itself, but finding smarter ways to manage complexity when designing solutions and architecting systems.
